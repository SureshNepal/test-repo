National Strategy

The Goal

"Bhutan achieves sustainability for its health sector and is established as a commercially successful regional genomics and wellness hub."

Strategic Partnerships
Strategic Context

Global Issues

1. Conflict in West Asia

Disrupted supply chains (reagents/equipment). A Biobank reduces dependency on emergency imports, 'insuring' Bhutan against supply shocks.

2. Poverty

Reduces national health costs through targeted drug procurement, ensuring 'Free Healthcare' remains sustainable.

3. Climate Change

Focuses on preservation of biodiversity (Natural Capital) as a tool for climate-resilient drug discovery.
