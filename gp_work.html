Establishment of Bhutan as a Regional Genomics Hub

Sustainability & Finance

Main Output

A Financial Sustainability Roadmap: A detailed guidebook that explains exactly how the biobank will charge fees, manage its budget, and save money for future requirements.

Activities for Financial Independence

  • Develop a comprehensive Unit-Costing Framework to determine service fees
  • A Digital Data & Billing Hub: A streamlined system connected to the lab and the Digital Health Platform (DHP) that allows you to share custom-curated datasets based on client needs, while automatically handling invoices and payment tracking.

The Regional Hub

Main Outputs

  • International Lab Accreditation (ISO): Getting an official "Seal of Quality" (like ISO 20387) that proves our biobank meets the highest global scientific standards.
  • An Investor Information Portal: A secure website or dashboard where international donors and private companies can see that Bhutan is a safe, high-quality place to invest their money.

Activities for the Regional Hub

  • Conduct a comprehensive ISO 20387 Gap Analysis and implement a National Staff Certification Program to align laboratory operations, quality control, and data security with international biobanking standards.
  • Brand Bhutan as a wellness tourism destination.
  • Portal Development: Build a secure, professional Investor Dashboard that displays real-time stats (e.g., number of samples, security uptime) to show donors that the facility is world-class.

Revenue Models & Commercialization

Strategic Output

Revenue models for biobank use cases (Bhutan Gene Bank, Traditional Medicine, Wellness Center) are fully operationalized.

Activities for Revenue Models

  • Develop a market-aligned Service Catalog supported by specialized Material Transfer Agreements (MTAs) and develop International Investment Roadmap to secure B2B partnerships and protect Bhutan’s data royalty rights.
  • Design and pilot wellness bundles for regional tourists.
  • Demonstration Research Support: Funding and supporting research processes from hypothesis verification to clinical research, often involving CROs and joint research courses.
